# Qualify Strands history policy for a protected execution

**RFC-0022 · Delivery status: Planned.** Keep `strands-box` opt-in and the
[RFC](../rfcs/0022-strands-history-policy/index.md) proposed until maintainers
decide it. The [implementation under review](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw-enterprise/pull/2543)
is the starting point. Earlier controlled Linux results apply to the source and
placement exercised there; repeat the installed checks on the version we intend to ship.

1. **Admit the installation.** Pin the reviewed Box, policy engine and terminal
   exchange hook. Use the normal controller and worker permissions. An absent
   or stale installation profile must stop source adoption before a credential
   read; an unqualified node must not receive the workload or sender.
2. **Follow one execution.** On an enforcing Kubernetes installation, observe
   the signed release, one native startup and one user turn at a controlled
   origin. Verify both telemetry omissions and that routine maintenance adds no
   provider request. Count an attempt before credential access; if durable
   history is unavailable, stop before sending.
3. **Exercise the failure boundaries.** Check direct and neighboring bypass,
   an added network grant and a separately admitted execution with zero routes.
   Withdraw authority between resolution and send; reject a forbidden final
   trailer; lose a reply and observe no replay. Correlate each result with what
   the origin actually received, including when that outcome remains unknown.
4. **Retire, then decide where to enable it.** Stop the sender and confirm
   workload removal before releasing the fence or disposing of history. Keep
   live route reduction unavailable until it preserves that history and the
   remaining routes. Security reviewers and installation owners assess the
   exact build, network, node, metadata and storage boundaries for each placement.

Use the [source flow](../../docs/flows/strands-box-protected-execution.md)
to trace a failure and the [qualification guide](../../docs/testing/strands-box-credential-proxy.md)
to collect the evidence. Cloud placement and provider behavior need their own qualification.
